### A useful way to categorize these 30

For your **ai-lab.in/temples** project, I wouldn't put all 30 into a single "temples" category. I'd use something like:

| Category                               | Examples                                                                    | Approx. number |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------: |
| 🛕 **Active major pilgrimage temples** | Shankaracharya, Kheer Bhawani, Amarnath, Sharika, Mamaleshwar               |            ~10 |
| 🛕 **Active/restored local temples**   | Jwala Ji, Bhadrakali, Raghunath, Kapalmochan, Vichar Nag                    |             ~8 |
| 🏛️ **Ancient surviving temples**      | Pandrethan, Buniyar, Payar, Ladhoo                                          |             ~4 |
| 🏛️ **Ruined archaeological temples**  | Martand, Avantiswami, Avantisvara, Wangath, Shankaragaurishvara, Sugandhesa |             ~8 |

This distinction is important because **Martand, for example, should not be presented on your website as though it were a functioning temple today**. It is primarily an archaeological/heritage site, although religious activity has occurred there on special occasions. The ASI is currently undertaking conservation/documentation planning for several Kashmir monuments, including Martand, Naranag and Buniyar. ([Press Information Bureau][25])
